    Where this city comes from

    Every shape traces to an open dataset, packed once into the page. Nothing is fetched from a map server while you play, and nothing was traced from Google imagery.

    Real lot lines, estimated details. Lot lines, parcel numbers and zoning are real City of LA data, trimmed where they meet the street model. LA County Assessor records (use code, year built, units) and the county's 2023 building heights were not reachable (the county servers refuse cloud requests), so lot type comes from building use, size and zone, building heights come from Overture, and build year and unit counts are guesses except where a recent city building permit gives the year. Those are marked "est." in the inspector. Yard and street trees are placed by rule. Owner names and sale prices are never shown.

    Real rules are simplified. Each zone family uses one set of setbacks, a height cap and a list of allowed lot uses. Hillside rules, specific plans and overlays are not modelled. Roofs are built on rectangles rather than a full straight skeleton.
