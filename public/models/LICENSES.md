# Model and data licences

No 3D model files are bundled yet. Building looks are generated from open data:

- Overture Maps buildings and places (2026-10-21.0): OpenStreetMap (ODbL), Microsoft ML Buildings (ODbL). Attribution is in the area pack's `sources`.
- Overhead imagery (optional, `tools/looks/build_looks.py --fetch-naip`): USGS NAIP, public domain.
- Not used, on purpose: Google, Apple and Bing imagery or 3D tiles (their terms forbid extracting from them).

Any model added later must be CC0 or CC-BY and be listed here with its source URL and licence.
